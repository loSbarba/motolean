Delete your MotoLean account

Draft under review: text in square brackets is still to be completed.

You can delete your MotoLean account and all related data in two ways.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open MotoLean and go to Profile.
  2. In Account and privacy tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete account and online data.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email samusbar@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete MotoLean account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm when it is done. We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you.

What is deleted

The following is permanently deleted from our server:

  • your account (email and Google sign-in);
  • your profile: name, @nickname, photo, city, licence year;
  • your garage;
  • all uploaded rides, including tracks, statistics and sensor data files;
  • friendships, reactions, comments, challenge and group ride participation, curve and road efforts, ratings and roads you published.

The server provider’s backup copies are overwritten within [7] days.

What stays

  • Rides saved on your phone stay on the phone until you delete them from the Sessions list or remove the app.
  • We keep no other data after deletion.
