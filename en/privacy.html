This policy explains which personal data the MotoLean app (iPhone and Android) processes, why, where it is stored and what your rights are, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Italian law (Legislative Decree 196/2003).

1. Data controller

Samuele Sbarbaro, Bollate (MI), Italy. Privacy contact: samusbar@gmail.com

2. In short

  • You can use MotoLean without an account: in that case no data leaves your phone.
  • If you create an account, some data is stored on our server in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) for syncing and social features.
  • Every ride starts private (“Only me”): you decide whether to share it with friends or with everyone.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

3. Data that stays on your phone only

Without an account, and partly with one, this data stays on the device and we never receive it:

  • recorded rides (motion sensor data, GPS track, speed, lean angle);
  • local profile and garage, and the location of your Privacy zone: kept in an encrypted file (AES-256) whose key stays in the phone’s keychain;
  • the emergency contact for crash detection (name and number picked from your contacts);
  • app settings.

We do not process special categories of data (such as health data). Crash detection runs on the phone only: when it triggers, you (or whoever helps you) send the SMS to your emergency contact from the Messages app, and it never goes through our servers.

5. Who sees your data

  • Other registered users: your public profile (name, @nickname, photo, city, licence year).
  • Friends: your garage (read-only), rides set to “Friends”, totals (distance, number of rides, max lean) in your profile and on leaderboards.
  • All registered users: rides set to “Everyone”, roads you publish, ratings and comments.
  • Group ride members: your live location while you stay in the group (12 hours at most). It is deleted when you leave.
  • Your email is never shown to other users.

6. Providers (processors)

  • Supabase Inc.: database, authentication and file storage. Data is stored in the EU region in Frankfurt (Germany). Supabase works under a data processing agreement (DPA) including the Standard Contractual Clauses for any access from outside the EU.
  • Google: only if you choose “Sign in with Google”. Google is an independent controller for your Google account data: see its privacy policy.
  • Apple / Google (app stores): app download and any purchases, under their own privacy policies.
  • Maps: maps are provided by Apple Maps (iPhone) and OpenStreetMap (Android), which receive the requests for the map tiles to display.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and content: until you delete your account.
  • Group ride location: until you leave the group, 12 hours at most.
  • When you delete your account we delete from the server your account, profile, photo, garage, rides (including raw data files), friendships and related content. The provider’s backup copies are overwritten within [7] days.

8. Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to samusbar@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

  • Delete your account: in the app, Profile › Delete account and online data, or from the Delete account page.
  • Export your rides: in the app, as GPX or JSON.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or the authority of your country.

9. Minimum age

MotoLean is intended for people aged 14 or over. You cannot create an account under that age.

10. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on every database table (each user sees only what they are allowed to), encrypted local data on the phone, sign-in credentials in the system keychain.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
