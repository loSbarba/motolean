2. What MotoLean does

MotoLean records your motorcycle rides with the phone’s sensors and GPS, computes statistics such as lean angle, speed and route, and offers optional social features (friends, feed, leaderboards, challenges, group rides, roads).

3. Safety first

  • Do not use your phone while riding. Start and check the app only when stopped. Fix the phone to a suitable mount.
  • Always obey traffic laws and speed limits. Leaderboards, challenges, records and “legendary curves” are for fun and comparison, not an invitation to race. Riding at the limit belongs on a track, in an organised and safe setting.
  • Measured values are approximate. Lean angle, speed and other data depend on the phone, the mount and the calibration and may contain errors. Do not use them for safety decisions or to set up your bike.
  • Crash detection is an aid, not a certified safety device. It may miss a crash or report a false one. It only works if the phone is intact, switched on, has signal and the app is running. The SMS to your emergency contact needs a tap on “Send”. It does not replace emergency services or your phone’s own emergency features.
